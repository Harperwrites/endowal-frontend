Money decisions start early. Students deserve a place to practice before those decisions carry real consequences.

Meet Endowal Edu: financial learning through classroom practice.

Students connect assignments with simulated deposits and expenses, giving their money decisions a purpose.

They explore budgets and credit lessons, and practice the everyday details, like writing a check.

Teachers review submitted work, record grades, and share feedback, keeping the learning connected.

Schools and programs can make the experience their own with customized branding.
