## Recording and editing direction

- Use the landing page's interactive demo for the student-to-teacher sequence. Record only fictional demonstration accounts and content.
- Label the app footage **“Product demo · simulated money”** legibly throughout the product sequence. Label the school branding shot **“Example school customization.”**
- Capture at 1920×1080 or higher. Keep cursor movement deliberate; allow each featured action time to read. Avoid fast scrolling.
- Use warm instrumental music licensed for fundraising and commercial use. Keep it below the narration; provide burned-in captions plus a separate caption file in the finished edit.
- Favor gentle zooms, clean cuts, and the app's existing colors. End on a working demo/contact destination selected for publication; do not place a localhost address in the public video.
- The invitation expresses a fundraising goal. Do not add unverified adoption figures, learning outcomes, testimonials, school partnerships, investment returns, or tax-deductibility claims.
